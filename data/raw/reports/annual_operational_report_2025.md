# Niiha Technologies — FY 2025 Annual Performance & Risk Report

## Executive Summary
In FY 2025, Niiha Technologies achieved total consolidated revenue of **$18.4M**, representing a **28.5% YoY growth** over FY 2024 ($14.32M). Gross margin expanded from 71.2% to **74.8%**, driven by automated multi-tenant cluster provisioning in **Niiha Cloud Core**.

## Segment Performance Breakdown
1. **Niiha Cloud Core (58% of Revenue | $10.67M):** Strongest retention segment with a Net Dollar Retention (NDR) of 118%. Primary infrastructure cost is AWS/GCP reserved compute.
2. **Niiha AI Analytics (29% of Revenue | $5.34M):** Fastest-growing product line (+64% YoY), though gross margins remain lower (66%) due to high inference token costs.
3. **Enterprise Advisory Services (13% of Revenue | $2.39M):** Implementation, custom RAG deployment, and legacy migration consulting.

## Key Operational Risks & Audit Findings (Q4 2025)
- **Discount Leakage:** An internal audit found that 14% of Q4 deals in the EMEA and APAC regions exceeded autonomous sales discount caps without documented VP or CFO sign-off.
- **Receivables Aging:** Accounts Receivable (AR) > 60 days rose to $620,000 at year-end, concentrated heavily among mid-market manufacturing clients.
- **FY 2026 Strategic Targets:** Reduce unapproved discount leakage to under 2%, maintain overall EBITDA margin above 18%, and cap cloud inference COGS at 28% of AI Analytics revenue.
