# Niiha Technologies — Revenue Recognition & Enterprise Discount Policy (FY 2026)

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs contract pricing, maximum sales discount thresholds, and revenue recognition rules for Niiha’s three core business lines: **Niiha Cloud Core**, **Niiha AI Analytics**, and **Enterprise Advisory Services**.

## 2. Standard Discount Tiers & Approval Matrix
Sales representatives may apply standard discounts based on Annual Contract Value (ACV) and contract duration:
- **Tier 1 (ACV under $50,000):** Maximum autonomous discount is **10%**. Discounts between 10.1% and 15% require Regional Sales Manager approval.
- **Tier 2 (ACV $50,000 – $249,999):** Maximum autonomous discount is **15%**. Discounts up to 22% require VP of Sales sign-off.
- **Tier 3 (ACV $250,000+):** Maximum autonomous discount is **20%**. Any discount exceeding **25%** is classified as a **Margin Exception** and requires CFO approval prior to contract execution.
- **Multi-Year Bonus:** Multi-year agreements (24 or 36 months) qualify for an additional **5%** discount stackable on top of the tier cap without escalating approval, provided upfront annual billing is selected.

## 3. Non-Standard Payment & Churn Risk Clauses
- Invoices are issued on **Net-30** terms by default. **Net-60** or **Net-90** terms reduce recognized Q1/Q2 cash-flow weight by 15% in internal financial reporting.
- Any client account with **2 or more overdue invoices** exceeding 45 days past due is automatically flagged as `High Credit Risk` and restricted from license expansions.
- **High-Value Churn Alert:** If an Enterprise client (ACV ≥ $100,000) shows a **greater than 30% drop** in monthly active API calls over two consecutive months, Customer Success must log a `Retention Intervention` ticket.
